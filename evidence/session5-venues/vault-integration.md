# Proposed Spark Savings account extension

A future account version could hold Spark Savings USDC shares on X Layer 196 directly. The current v2 ABI and app have no vault action. The candidate is spUSDC `0xf90E63079D97a0A1f479b2b168457F420CAFf6ba` with native Circle USDC `0xB6CEceAB302E2E4948951eE7843FC24E92933061`. Both use six decimals. At block 76,931,173, 100 USDC previewed 99.349568 spUSDC and redeeming those shares previewed 99.999999 USDC. Immediate cash was 10 million USDC against 52.83 million USDC of accounting assets. [Pinned reads](observations.json) · [Spark registry](https://github.com/sparkdotfi/spark-address-registry/blob/28a6ef4eccb404313ae2b111f8ef04f4e2018183/src/XLayer.sol)

**Proposed account methods** are `depositToVault(uint256 assets,uint256 minShares,uint64 deadline)` and `redeemFromVault(uint256 shares,uint256 minAssets,uint64 deadline)`. The deployment binds one vault and one underlying, checks `vault.asset()` and records the implementation and its governance assumptions. Neither method accepts a route, destination, arbitrary bytes or share owner. The account calls the verified vault directly. Deposits consume idle USDC with an exact temporary allowance, mint shares to the account and clear the allowance. Redemptions pass the account as both owner and recipient. They require no share approval. Check exact input balance changes and reconcile the observed output delta, vault return value and caller minimum atomically. Reject zero outputs and expired requests.

A deposit grant would scope the operator, venue, operation, USDC asset, per-call limit, cumulative budget and expiry. Existing swap execution inputs do not describe a vault allocation. A future vault policy must explicitly budget deposit exposure and carry a reviewed minimum-share bound derived from independently reviewed rate/valuation rules. A redeem grant would use raw share units and pay USDC only to the account. Keep owner redemption available through a circuit outage up to the actual vault liquidity and enforce the owner's minimum. Report liquid idle assets, vault shares, redeemable assets at current `maxRedeem(account)`, and an unavailable redemption separately. Assets deposited are no longer idle collateral or lending-book shares.

**Permissions and liquidity** matter to settlement. Spark's public deposit checks its cap and bars both a depositor and receiver with `TAKER_ROLE`. Its `maxDeposit` checks remaining cap only. Its default admin can change the cap, set rate bounds and upgrade the UUPS implementation. A setter changes the savings rate within those bounds, and a taker can draw actual USDC out of the vault. `maxWithdraw(owner)` returns the smaller of actual cash and the owner's claim. `maxRedeem(owner)` bounds shares by cash and ownership. A preview and a successful simulation can both change before inclusion. Treat cash availability and the difference between issuer USD exposure and vault credit exposure as explicit constraints. [Pinned vault source](https://github.com/sparkdotfi/spark-vaults-v2/blob/0a686ba/src/SparkVault.sol)

**Acceptance before enablement** would require a pinned 196 fork deposit and redeem with observed account balances, zero temporary allowances, authorization and min-out denials, depleted-liquidity handling, cap and policy failure, repeatable accounting and UI reconciliation. A 1952 inventory-backed vault fixture would need independent deployment and a visible testnet label. The proposed interface is planning evidence only.
